Page title:
Yelp reviews of Knox College

Page summary:
Name: Knox College
Location: Galesburg, Illinois

Review text:
- A parent visiting with their son said Knox had a wonderful campus with historic buildings that appeared well maintained. They were impressed by student ambassadors and professors who made themselves available during the visit. Their son was also impressed by the students and professors.

- A student from the West Coast said Knox is worthwhile and unique. They described the campus as small, which makes it easy to make friends across ages and backgrounds and to get to class on time. They said the classes are compelling, engaging, and challenging, and that faculty and staff are supportive inside and outside the classroom.

- The same student said Knox makes it easy to get involved through volunteer opportunities, language and cultural clubs, athletics, and performing arts. They also liked Galesburg because it has stores and restaurants students may need, including Walmart, Target, Baked Pizza, and Landmark Cafe. They noted that Knox is close to an Amtrak station with trains to Chicago.

- The same student described Knox as a close-knit and diverse community of scholars from across the United States and the world. They valued the friendships they made and expected those relationships to continue after graduation.

- A recent graduate said Knox is special because of the people, including students, professors, faculty, and coaches. They said people know each other, say hello, and make the campus feel welcoming and home-like. They described graduation as meaningful because the community gathered to say goodbye and good luck.

- One reviewer listed two major downsides: living in a small town was hard, and a small campus could feel socially cramped. Even with those drawbacks, they praised the professors for caring about students and said small classes were valuable. They felt Knox was less cliquey than larger schools and that Greek and non-Greek students, athletes and non-athletes, often mixed socially.

- One alum said Knox gave them an excellent education, though it was expensive. They praised the professors, study abroad programs, and classroom environment. They said Knox helped them learn to speak up in class and embrace Spanish. They also warned that a tiny college can create drama, and that Greek versus non-Greek tension was becoming more intense during their time.

- One negative reviewer said Knox was overpriced for a mediocre liberal arts education. They criticized outdated facilities, some professors, limited course options within majors, old classrooms and dorms, Galesburg, and dining hall food. They argued students should consider saving money and going elsewhere.

- One alum said they loved Knox and Galesburg. They described Knox as a midwestern liberal arts school with some quirky professors and students but said that was limited. They said Galesburg has real problems but also many genuinely great people. They liked that everyone knew each other, while acknowledging that closeness can feel intense.

- One alum said the average Knox student seemed less sheltered and less wealthy than students at some other liberal arts schools. They remembered many Midwestern students, along with students from places like Seattle and Portland. They said they were fond of Knox and wanted to balance out a prior low review.

- Another alum said Knox made living in a small central Illinois town worthwhile. They said Knox gave them a strong education, a meaningful college experience, and a lasting sense of connection to coaches, professors, friends, and the broader community.

- One reviewer described a mixed experience. At times they disliked the small town and small school, but at other times they valued the professors and opportunities. Overall, they were glad to attend because the education was strong, they met good people, and the experience was valuable. Their main complaint was wishing Knox were closer to Chicago.
